## How it works

**1. Connect your tools.** Link GitHub, your calendar, and a problem-solving platform. Two minutes. We do not read your code, your event descriptions, or your messages — we only see the shape of your work.

**2. The work speaks.** We watch commits, contest submissions, and consistency. You focus on building. We update your Skill Proof Score in the background, every time you push.

**3. Share the proof.** A credential that any recruiter can verify in ten seconds. No portfolio. No PDF. No "please find my resume attached". One link, one QR code, one signed record.

## What makes Antarix different

- W3C-standard credentials, not PDFs
- Verified by signal, not self-report
- WhatsApp-native — works on any phone, no app needed
- Privacy-first: you decide what is shared and with whom
- Free for students. Paid for recruiters and colleges.
