## 2. Purpose limitation (Section 4)

Personal data is processed only for the following purposes, communicated to you at sign-up and at each new opt-in:

- Operating your Antarix account; computing your Skill Proof Score and placement prediction
- Issuing and verifying your exportable Skill Proof credential
- Delivering AI Coach nudges you have opted into
- Enabling college and company features you have opted into
- Security, fraud prevention, and abuse detection
- Aggregated, anonymized analytics (cohort size, language trends)

We do not process your personal data for any purpose not listed above. We do not sell personal data.

## 3. Consent (Sections 6 and 7)

- **Explicit consent** is required for WhatsApp nudges, web push, and any optional surface (Calendar, Power Mode) you connect. You grant it in the dashboard or browser prompt and withdraw it at any time from **Settings → Privacy**.
- **Deemed consent** under Section 7 covers the minimum data needed to deliver the core service (account, Skill Proof Score, credential).
- Consent from a child below 18 is **not valid** under Section 9. We are an 18+ service and delete any under-18 account within 7 days of discovery.

## 4. Your rights (Sections 11–14)

You have the right to **access**, **correct**, and **erase** your personal data (subject to §5), to **grievance redressal** (write to the Grievance Officer; we respond within 30 days), and to **nominate** another individual to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity. To exercise any of these, go to **Settings → Privacy** in the app, or email `privacy@antarix.app`.

## 5. Retention

We retain personal data for the periods listed in [Privacy Notice](privacy-notice.md) §6. Anonymized aggregates that cannot be linked back to you may be retained indefinitely.

## 6. Cross-border transfer (Section 16)

Personal data of Indian data principals may be transferred outside India only to a country not on the **negative list** issued by the Central Government under Section 16(2). All current processors (Supabase, Meta, Google, GitHub) operate in jurisdictions not currently on that list. ## 7. Breach notification (Section 8(6))

In the event of a personal-data breach that is likely to cause harm to you, we will notify the **Data Protection Board of India within 72 hours** of becoming aware of it, under the rules notified under Section 8(6), and notify affected data principals without unreasonable delay.
